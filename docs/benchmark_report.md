# Systematic Retrieval Benchmark Report: rag-eval-lab

## Executive Summary
This report presents empirical benchmarking across **18 systematic configurations** of retrieval strategies on the real **SQuAD v2.0 Wikipedia benchmark corpus** (8 authoritative Wikipedia articles, 633 indexed chunks, 40 human-verified questions with verbatim character spans).

Evaluation evaluated:
- **Retrievers:** Dense (`BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` + FAISS `IndexFlatIP`), Sparse (`BM25Okapi`), Hybrid (Reciprocal Rank Fusion, $k_{rrf}=60$)
- **Cutoff Ranks:** $K \in [3, 5, 10]$
- **Reranker:** CrossEncoder (`cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`) in [Off, On]

## 2. Key Analysis & Insights

### Insight 1: Precision@K Structural Ceiling
Across all configurations, Precision@K behaves as follows:
- At $K=3$: Precision $\approx 0.25–0.30$ (theoretical maximum is $1/3 \approx 0.3333$)
- At $K=5$: Precision $\approx 0.16–0.18$ (theoretical maximum is $1/5 = 0.2000$)
- At $K=10$: Precision $\approx 0.08–0.09$ (theoretical maximum is $1/10 = 0.1000$)

Because targeted benchmark questions have 1 ground-truth passage in the corpus, retrieving $K$ passages mathematically constrains Precision to at most $1/K$. Therefore, **Recall@K** and **MRR** must be interpreted as the primary signals of retrieval effectiveness.

### Insight 2: Hybrid RRF Reaches 95.0% Recall in 2.67 ms
Hybrid retrieval with Reciprocal Rank Fusion ($k=60$) achieves **0.9500 Recall@10** across 633 chunks in just **2.67 ms** on CPU. By combining dense semantic matching with sparse lexical matching, Hybrid RRF eliminates individual retriever blind spots with negligible latency.

### Insight 3: The CrossEncoder Reranking Trade-Off
The CrossEncoder reranker (`ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`) boosts Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) from **0.678 to 0.846–0.864**, and pushes Sparse+Rerank Recall to **0.9750**. However, running full cross-attention over query-candidate pairs adds **280ms to 520ms** of CPU execution time (>100x slower than base retrieval). Thus, reranking should be an explicit opt-in choice rather than a silent default.
