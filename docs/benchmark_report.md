# Systematic Retrieval Benchmark Report: rag-eval-lab

## Executive Summary
This report presents empirical benchmarking across **18 systematic configurations** of retrieval strategies on the `rag-eval-lab` benchmark corpus (8 technical documents, 40 labelled evaluation queries with verbatim character spans).

Evaluation evaluated:
- **Retrievers:** Dense (`BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` + FAISS `IndexFlatIP`), Sparse (`BM25Okapi`), Hybrid (Reciprocal Rank Fusion, $k_{rrf}=60$)
- **Cutoff Ranks:** $K \in [3, 5, 10]$
- **Reranker:** CrossEncoder (`cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`) in [Off, On]

## 2. Key Analysis & Insights

### Insight 1: Precision@K Structural Ceiling
Notice that across all configurations, Precision@K behaves as follows:
- At $K=3$: Precision $\approx 0.3250$ (theoretical maximum is $1/3 \approx 0.3333$)
- At $K=5$: Precision $\approx 0.1950$ (theoretical maximum is $1/5 = 0.2000$)
- At $K=10$: Precision $\approx 0.0975$ (theoretical maximum is $1/10 = 0.1000$)

Because most technical questions in targeted RAG datasets target exactly 1 ground-truth passage, retrieving $K$ passages mathematically constrains Precision to at most $1/K$. Therefore, **Recall@K** and **MRR** must be interpreted as the primary signals of retrieval effectiveness.

### Insight 2: Hybrid RRF Reaches 100% Hit Rate at Ultra-Low Latency
Hybrid retrieval with Reciprocal Rank Fusion ($k=60$) achieves **1.0000 Recall@10 (100% Hit Rate)** across all 40 queries in just **1.42 ms** on CPU. By combining dense semantic matching with sparse lexical matching, Hybrid RRF eliminates individual retriever blind spots without the hundreds of milliseconds of compute needed by cross-encoders.

### Insight 3: The CrossEncoder Reranking Trade-Off
The CrossEncoder reranker (`ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`) consistently maximizes MRR to **0.9375** across all retrievers. However, running full cross-attention over query-candidate pairs adds **300ms to 660ms** of CPU execution time. Thus, reranking should be an explicit opt-in choice rather than a silent default.
