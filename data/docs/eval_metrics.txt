# RAG Evaluation Metrics & Benchmarking

Evaluating RAG systems requires distinct metrics for the retrieval module and the generation component. Retrieval evaluation measures whether the index successfully finds relevant information, while generation evaluation measures the quality and truthfulness of the final LLM response.

## Retrieval Metrics: Recall@k & MRR
Recall@k measures the proportion of relevant ground-truth passages or character spans that are successfully retrieved within the top-k results. In span-based evaluation, a hit is scored if the character offsets of any retrieved chunk overlap with the ground-truth character span in the source document.

Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) evaluates the quality of ranking order. It calculates the reciprocal of the 1-based rank at which the first relevant hit appears (1 / rank). If the first hit is at rank 1, MRR is 1.0; if at rank 2, MRR is 0.5; and if no relevant chunk is retrieved in top-k, MRR is 0.0.

## Generation Metrics: LLM-as-Judge Faithfulness
Faithfulness evaluates whether the answer generated by an LLM is completely supported by the retrieved context chunks, without hallucinating unverified claims. In an LLM-as-judge setup, the generated response is decomposed into individual atomic claims. Each claim is checked against the retrieved text chunks and labeled as either supported or unsupported. The faithfulness score is calculated as the ratio of supported claims to total claims.
